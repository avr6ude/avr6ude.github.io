Faceoff privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Faceoff is a pack of two-player games played on one phone. You don't create an account and we never ask for your name, email or contacts. This page explains the little data the app does handle.

What the app collects

Gameplay analytics. When you play, the app records events such as opening the app, starting and finishing a match, which game was played, who won, the final score, rematches and ads shown. Each event carries a random ID created on your device, the app version, your device model, operating system and language. We use this to see which games people enjoy, find bugs and decide how often ads appear. Analytics are processed by PostHog in the European Union. IP addresses are discarded on arrival.

Crash reports. If the app hits an error, a report with the error and the same device details is sent to PostHog so we can fix it.

Ads. Faceoff is free and shows an ad after some matches, served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first; you can change it at any time from your device's settings or by reinstalling the app. On iPhone, the app only uses the advertising ID if you allow tracking when asked. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't collect names, emails, phone numbers, photos, contacts or precise location. The app has no chat and no accounts.

Children

Faceoff is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

How long we keep it

Analytics events are kept for up to one year, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can refuse ad personalisation in the consent prompt or in your device settings, and you can delete all data stored on your phone by uninstalling the app. To ask us to delete the analytics linked to your device's random ID, or for any question about this policy, write to r@avrdu.de.

Who we are

Faceoff is made by Rauf Salikhov, Serbia.
